Our Story

Many families make amazing things at home, but very few people see them.

These families sell through social media, and their customers are usually in the same city.

We created Artizan to connect these families with customers. The hand in our logo means work made by hand with care.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To help home-based families sell their handmade products online.

Our Vision

To be the first trusted place in Saudi Arabia for handmade products.

Our Goals

  1. Give home-based families one website to sell on.
  2. Help local sellers reach more customers.
  3. Increase the income of small home businesses.
  4. Make ordering easy for customers.
  5. Build trust with ratings and reviews.
