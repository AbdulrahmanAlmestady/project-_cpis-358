What is Artizan?

Many families make beautiful products at home, but they can not reach customers outside their area.

Most of them sell only on social media. Orders are written by hand in chats, and mistakes and delays happen a lot.

Artizan gives these families one website to show their products, receive orders and get ratings from customers.

Who Benefits from Artizan?

Home-Based Families

  • Show their products in one place.
  • Reach customers in other cities.
  • Manage orders easily.
  • Get a more stable income.

Customers

  • Find real handmade products.
  • See clear prices and photos.
  • Read reviews from other buyers.
  • Support local families.

How It Works

  1. Join: the family creates a free account.
  2. Add: they upload photos and prices of their products.
  3. Order: customers choose products and order them.
  4. Review: the buyer gets the order and leaves a rating.
